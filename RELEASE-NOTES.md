# PGO version 2.0, release notes

Release date: November 25, 2026

## What is new

1. **35 core concepts** (version 1.0 had 17): `PGO-00000001` to `PGO-00000036`, without `PGO-00000034`.
2. **A SKOS vocabulary** for annotation, search, and cross-system mapping, with one technical top concept so the list can be loaded into terminology portals.
3. **Concept pages rewritten** with a common structure: definition, source and license, preferred sources, examples, synonyms, external mappings, and contributors.
4. **PGO is now in the Pistoia Alliance namespace** `https://spec.pistoiaalliance.org/PGO/ontology/`.
5. **Sources and instances verified** for all concepts through expert recommendation, review and a systematic check of the example links.
6. **Consent-based acknowledgment.** Experts are named on the concept pages they contributed to only when they agreed. Everyone else appears as "Anonymous" with the role kept. The list of experts in `experts/` follows the same rule. Contributors to version 1.0 remain on the list.

## What did not change

- The identifiers of the 17 concepts from version 1.0.

## Not included

- `PGO-00000034` (Digital_Therapeutic_Product) is approved but not published yet. Its definition comes from ISO/TR 11147:2023 and the publisher has not yet confirmed permission to reuse it.
- Relations and axioms between concepts. They are planned for Phase 3.

## License notes

CC BY 4.0, except where a concept states another license. Molecular_Target uses a Drug Target Ontology definition under CC BY-SA 4.0.
