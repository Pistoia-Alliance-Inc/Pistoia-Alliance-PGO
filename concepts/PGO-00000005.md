# Definition *(skos:definition)*

Research conducted with human subjects or on material of human origin in which an investigator directly interacts with human subjects; includes development of new technologies, study of mechanisms of human diseases, therapy, clinical trials, epidemiologic, behavior, and health services research.

# Editorial notes

## Summary Expert Alignment *(dcterms:description)*

**Round 1** (2024-07-03)

The session established initial consensus around NCIT C15206. Key discussion points:

- **Common identifier source**: AstraZeneca emphasised the importance of agreeing on which identifier source to use for clinical trials to avoid ambiguity in cross-system referencing.
- **Resolution services**: GSK proposed a preference hierarchy for resolution services (e.g., identifiers.org first, then alternatives). This was noted for future preferred-sources discussion.
- **Preference for original source text**: Merck KGaA expressed a preference for using the definition text exactly as found in the original reference resource. This position was unanimously endorsed and adopted as a guiding principle for PGO definitions generally.

**Outcome:** Initial consensus on NCIT C15206. Follow-up session scheduled to address ClinOps alignment.

---

**Round 2** (2024-08-01)

This session addressed input from the Pistoia Alliance ClinOps group (GitHub issue #34), which utilises the USDM (Unified Study Definition Model) definition: *"involves research using human volunteers (also called participants) that is intended to add to medical knowledge."*

The expert group concluded that:
1. The USDM/ClinOps definition is not in conflict with NCIT_C15206; the NCIT definition is broader but compatible.
2. The second part of the NCIT definition ("includes development of new technologies, study of mechanisms of human diseases...") was viewed as descriptive commentary rather than essential semantic content.
3. The terminological equivalence of "human subject", "volunteer", and "participant" was acknowledged as an open point but not a critical obstacle to adoption.

**Outcome:** Confirmed consensus on NCIT_C15206. GitHub issue #34 resolved. Formal recommendation submitted to the Steering Group.
