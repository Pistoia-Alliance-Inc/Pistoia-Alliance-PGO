# Definition *(skos:definition)*

A sign, signal, circumstance, or symptom which serves to indicate or point out the cause, pathology, treatment, or issue of an attack of disease; the basis for initiation of a treatment for a disease or of a diagnostic test (causal, or symptomatic, or disease-specific indication).

# Editorial notes

## Summary Expert Alignment *(dcterms:description)*

**Round 1** (2024-09-11)

The session reviewed six candidate definitions for "indication". Several definitions were immediately excluded due to the absence of a resolvable URI. Two candidates remained viable:
1. **NCIT_C41184** — provides a resolvable IRI with CC BY 4.0 license.
2. **CDISC Glossary** — the CDISC definition is preferred by some experts for its clarity ("A health problem or disease that is identified as likely to be benefited by a therapy..."), but CDISC reuses the NCIT code C41184 with its own wording, and no separate URI exists for the CDISC-specific definition.

Discussion notes:
- "Abnormal" or "pathological" are unnecessarily restrictive when defining indication.
- The distinction between sign and symptom in the NCIT definition was noted.
- NCIT code C41184 may be acceptable, with all alternate definitions treated as equally valid.

The group reached consensus: adopt NCIT_C41184 as the primary definition, with all listed alternate definitions acknowledged as equally valid.

**Outcome:** Consensus on NCIT_C41184 as primary definition, CDISC definition as alternative label. Submitted to Steering Group.
