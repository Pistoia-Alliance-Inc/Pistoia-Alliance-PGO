# Definition *(skos:definition)*

The principle target on which the action happens. Examples: The patient in physical examination, a specimen in a lab observation. May also be a patient's family member (teaching) or a device or room (cleaning, disinfecting, housekeeping).

# Editorial notes

## Summary Expert Alignment *(dcterms:description)*

**Round 1 *(Phase 1 session)*** (2025-03-12)

The Phase 1 alignment evaluated definitions and labels for the concept "Subject-Person". The group concluded that "Study Subject" must be sufficiently general to encompass: human participants (clinical trials), animal models, environmental systems (e.g. soil, water, air), microbiome-related samples, and cell cultures. Label "Subject-Person" was rejected as too narrow; "Study_Subject" accepted as the replacement.

NCIT_C41189 ("A matter or an individual that is observed, analyzed, examined, investigated, experimented upon, or/and treated in the course of a particular study") was recommended. The group also proposed introducing Clinical_Study_Subject as a separate concept for clinical-domain specificity, adopting NCIT C70668.

Data provenance considerations: identifiers for Study_Subject should capture associations with study protocols, samples, datasets, and dates.

**Outcome:** Phase 1 recommendation: (1) Label "Study_Subject"; (2) NCIT_C41189; (3) Add Clinical_Study_Subject as separate concept. SG rejected at 2025-06-12 — definition must cover non-human pre-clinical subjects.

---

**Round 2** (2025-08-21)

The group tested candidate definitions against concrete examples of study subjects: a dog in a veterinary interventional study; a person in a clinical trial; a rat in a preclinical safety study; an aliquot of compound X in a stability study; a dataset in a study testing analytical workflow performance. Context was emphasised: "Clinical Research Study" narrows to Clinical Study or Clinical Trial.

The group discussed whether "subject" should shift to "participant" in clinical contexts (recognising patient agency) but concluded that "Study_Subject" is the more precise ontological term. The broader question of interoperability across pharma functions and knowledge domains was noted.

**Outcome:** No final decision. New candidate definitions gathered. Session continued at 2025-09-18.

---

**Round 3** (2025-09-18)

The group converged on the HL7 C1550501 definition ("The principle target on which the action happens") as the recommended definition. Philippe Rocca-Serra noted its alignment with an HL7 FHIR resource and that "action" covers both observation and intervention, applicable to any study from physical/chemical characterisation to clinical trials. Birgit Meldal: covers everything. An expert: "action" is too broad and unclear, but accepted the group consensus.

The definition's extensibility was seen as a strength: examples include a patient in physical examination, a specimen in a lab observation, a device as subject of maintenance — coverage extends to environmental systems, microbiome, cell cultures relevant to PGO's pre-clinical scope.

The group also confirmed: Clinical_Study_Subject (another concept) is to be modelled as a distinct subclass of Study_Subject.

**Outcome:** Recommendation to SG: adopt HL7 C1550501 for Study_Subject. Clinical_Study_Subject established as a separate Phase 2 concept.
