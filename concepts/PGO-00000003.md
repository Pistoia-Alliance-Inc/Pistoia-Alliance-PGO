# Definition *(skos:definition)*

Any material sample taken from a biological entity for testing, diagnostic, propagation, treatment or research purposes, including a sample obtained from a living organism or taken from the biological object after halting of all its life functions. Biospecimen can contain one or more components including but not limited to cellular molecules, cells, tissues, organs, body fluids, embryos, and body excretory products.

# Editorial notes

## Summary Expert Alignment *(dcterms:description)*

**Round 1** (2025-05-08)

The session focused on defining "biospecimen" in the context of pharmaceutical R&ED. Three definitions were considered.

The expert group agreed on key scoping principles:
- The concept applies specifically to R&ED in pharma.
- It explicitly excludes purely physical-chemical specimens or samples not derived from biological entities, thereby providing necessary conceptual precision.
- The concept broadly aligns with "sample" but is more targeted and appropriate for biomedical research involving biological material.

The NCIT_C70699 definition was the convergence candidate, recommended by three partner organizations and endorsed by (Peter McQuilton) and (Birgit Meldal). It was considered both comprehensive and appropriately scoped for PGO use.

**Outcome:** Consensus reached on NCIT_C70699. Formal recommendation submitted to the Steering Group.
