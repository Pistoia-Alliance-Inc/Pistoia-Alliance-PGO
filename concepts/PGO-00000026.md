# Definition *(skos:definition)*

Any instrument, apparatus, implement, machine, appliance, implant, reagent for in vitro use, software, material or other similar or related article, intended by the manufacturer to be used, alone or in combination for, one or more specific medical purpose(s).

# Editorial notes

## Summary Expert Alignment *(dcterms:description)*

**Round 1** (2025-10-16)

The session reviewed eight candidate definitions for Medical_Device. Geography-specific definitions (FDA, EMA) were rejected early as too restrictive for PGO's international scope. Very detailed multi-line definitions (ISO/IEC Guide 63, ISO 11615:2017) were acknowledged as comprehensive but rejected as too verbose for PGO use. FHIR Device was seen as too broad. SNOMED was rejected for lack of a definition text.

Two NCIT C16830 variants (NCI Glossary and CDISC formulation) were compared directly. The CDISC formulation was strongly preferred: the group noted it captures "the same qualities as ISO 11615 but much more concise." Darko Hric noted the NCI Glossary variant (shorter) was sufficient to distinguish Medical_Device from other devices, but the CDISC variant was more complete in explicitly covering software, implants, and in vitro reagents alongside instruments and apparatus.

The preferred CDISC definition was already accessible via NCIT C16830, providing a stable, dereferenceable URI.

**Outcome:** Consensus reached on the NCIT C16830 CDISC formulation. Label `Medical_Device` confirmed.
