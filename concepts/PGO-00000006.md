# Editorial notes

## Summary Expert Alignment *(dcterms:description)*

**Round 1** (2025-01-30)

The session revealed significant conceptual complexity around the term "site", which spans anatomical, geographic, institutional, and procedural domains. Experts distinguished between a high-level generic concept and more context-specific subtypes.

Key findings:
- **BFO_0000029 (Site)**: Philosophically rigorous but too abstract for practical PGO implementation. Some experts appreciated its generality; others considered it impractical.
- **OMG Site definition** ("place, setting, or context in which something is situated or to which something is, or may be, bound") was generally preferred by experts for its clarity and pragmatic scope — however, the IRI is unresolvable (issue #48), blocking immediate adoption.
- **NCIT_C70777 (Clinical Study Site)**: Strongly endorsed for representing real-world entities engaged in clinical research.

The experts recommended a stratified approach:
1. A generic "Site" — pending a resolvable URI for the OMG definition.
2. "Clinical_Study_Site" (NCIT_C70777) — adopt for Phase 1.
3. Additional Phase 2 concepts: "Study_Site", "Anatomical_Site", "Binding_Site".

**Outcome:** Further update required to confirm OMG IRI status and finalise the pragmatic recommendation.

---

**Round 2** (2025-05-28)

Follow-up confirmed that the OMG IRI issue (#48) could not be resolved: OMG IRIs are not currently resolvable, per input from Asiyah Yu Lin (Pistoia Alliance). As a consequence, the OMG "Site" definition cannot be used for PGO at this time.

The pragmatic proposal for Phase 1:
1. Defer a generic "Site" definition until a resolvable and implementable URI is available.
2. Adopt "Clinical_Study_Site" with NCIT_C70777 as the Phase 1 core concept.
3. Flag additional Phase 2 sub-concepts for subsequent alignment.

**Outcome:** Final recommendation confirmed. Submitted to Steering Group.
