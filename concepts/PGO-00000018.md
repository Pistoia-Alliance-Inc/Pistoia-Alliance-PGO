# Definition *(skos:definition)*

Any unfavorable or unintended disease, sign, or symptom (including an abnormal laboratory finding) that is temporally associated with the use of a medical treatment or procedure, and that may or may not be considered related to the medical treatment or procedure.

# Editorial notes

## Summary Expert Alignment *(dcterms:description)*

**Round 1** (2025-10-16)

The session (2025-10-16) focused on Adverse Event as the primary concept (Medical Device was also discussed — separate record another concept).

The group reviewed approximately 10 candidate definitions, removing exact duplicates during the call. The shortlist converged on two candidates: OAE_0000001 and NCIT C41331.

OAE_0000001 was judged concise and from an authoritative dedicated ontology for adverse events, but noted as potentially limited in scope and not updated since 2022. NCIT C41331 was preferred by the group for its content richness — it covers drug-drug interactions (noted by an expert: "takes into account drug-drug interaction as well"), applies across clinical, regulatory, and post-market contexts, and is from a highly trusted, broadly used source. Darko noted NCIT definitions are generally "wordy" but agreed on content quality.

Process issues also discussed:
- URL policy established: definitions must point to the original source organization, not BioPortal. BioPortal is acceptable for discovery but not as the primary citation.
- Ammar added SIDER (http://sideeffects.embl.de/) as an example resource for Adverse Event during the call.
- A "meta-level" context label (R&D vs. clinical) was requested by Chris Day and an expert, but noted by Giovanni as requiring an agreed pharma meta-model not yet available.

**Outcome:** Consensus reached on NCIT C41331 as the preferred definition with the label `Adverse_Event`. Formal recommendation submitted to Steering Group.
