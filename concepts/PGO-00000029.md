# Definition *(skos:definition)*

A material entity, such as native (gene product) protein, protein complex, microorganism, DNA, etc., that physically interacts with a therapeutic or prophylactic drug (with some binding affinity) and where this physical interaction is (at least partially) the cause of a (detectable) clinical effect.

# Editorial notes

## Summary Expert Alignment *(dcterms:description)*

**Round 1 *(Phase 1 session)*** (2025-04-10)

The alignment meeting evaluated proposed, existing ontology and controlled vocabulary definitions for "target" in pharma research and early development (R&ED). The group emphasised that "molecular target" is a more appropriate and precise term than the broader "target", particularly in R&ED contexts.

Multiple sources were reviewed: NCIT C16128 (NCI Thesaurus) — preferred despite lacking a formal definition and framing being limited to malignancies; BAO_0003064 — too specific to assay design; GO:0003674 (Gene Ontology) — strong definition of molecular function but limited to gene products, excludes DNA; DTO_00000004 (Drug Target Ontology) — appropriate for drug development but overly narrow for exploratory research.

The group noted that molecular target appears to function more as a "role" than as a material entity — consistent with other PGO concepts such as Drug and Active Ingredient, which also carry role-based semantics.

**Outcome:** Recommendation to SG: (1) Replace "target" with "molecular_target" as the label; (2) Provisionally adopt NCIT C16128; (3) Consider minting a new, role-aware definition in the future.

---

**Round 2 *(Phase 2 session)*** (2025-09-04)

The group reviewed the pool of candidate definitions following the SG's rejection of NCIT C16128 (Phase 1). Two viable candidates emerged:

**Option 1 (preferred):** DTO_00000004 — "A material entity, such as native (gene product) protein, protein complex, microorganism, DNA, etc., that physically interacts with a therapeutic or prophylactic drug (with some binding affinity) and where this physical interaction is (at least partially) the cause of a (detectable) clinical effect." Favoured by (Darko Hric), Ammar Ammar, an expert, and for its good coverage of entity types and explicit causal mechanism. Giovanni flagged the label mismatch (Drug_Target vs. Molecular_Target).

**Option 2 (alternative):** NCIT C16128 — retained as a fallback but considered less suitable given SG's prior rejection and oncology framing.

Open issues raised but not fully resolved: (1) the DTO definition covers drug-interacting targets but not vaccine development contexts (e.g. antigen); (2) pre-pathological cell targets not clearly covered; (3) DTO maintenance concern (not updated since 2018, not in OLS).

**Outcome:** Recommendation to SG: adopt DTO_00000004 as working definition, acknowledging limitations; request label alignment to "Molecular_Target". Open issues to be noted as pending future definition refinement.
