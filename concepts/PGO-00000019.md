# Editorial notes

## Summary Expert Alignment *(dcterms:description)*

**Round 1** (2026-01-15)

The session (2026-01-15) covered Excipient as the first concept (API was the second concept, no consensus reached — see another concept).

Multiple candidates were reviewed. The discussion quickly narrowed to two finalists: ChEBI CHEBI_75324 and DRON:00000029.

The ChEBI definition ("A generally pharmacologically inactive substance that is formulated with the active ingredient of a medication") was acknowledged as a "negative" definition — it defines excipient by what it is not (not the active ingredient). Despite this limitation, the group converged on it for three reasons: (1) an expert/Roche confirmed ChEBI is the definition adopted by ISO IDMP, giving PGO-IDMP interoperability out of the box; (2) Birgit/Pfizer confirmed the same ("authoritative source & adopted by IDMP-O"); (3) it is concise and from a well-maintained, resolvable source.

The DRON definition was positively framed (listing what excipients do: aid manufacture, prolong shelf life, ensure proper administration), which Darko/Novo Nordisk acknowledged is semantically better. However, the phrase "scattered molecular aggregate" was universally criticised as impractical. The IDMP-O and EMA definitions were noted as essentially equivalent to ChEBI or weaker, and did not advance. The EMA definition ("a constituent of a medicine other than the active substance") was considered by some as simpler but without strong reason to prefer it over ChEBI.

**Outcome:** Consensus reached on ChEBI CHEBI_75324 as the preferred definition with the label `Excipient`. Formal recommendation submitted to Steering Group.
