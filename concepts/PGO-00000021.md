# Definition *(skos:definition)*

A broad term that includes all diseases, lesions, disorders, or nonpathologic condition that normally receives medical treatment, such as pregnancy or childbirth. Any kind of illness or circumstance which requires medical monitoring.

# Editorial notes

## Summary Expert Alignment *(dcterms:description)*

**Round 1** (2025-11-14)

The 2025-11-26 session (following initial discussion on 2025-11-14) reviewed seven candidate definitions. The central debate was the scope boundary between Disease and Medical_Condition: the expert group agreed that Medical_Condition must be broader than Disease, explicitly covering non-pathological conditions such as pregnancy, childbirth, and vaccination recommendations.

Key discriminators discussed: (a) non-pathological inclusion — schema.org was criticised for missing this; (b) treatment vs. monitoring — NCIT C156809 was rejected for limiting to "monitoring"; (c) circularity — OPMI and GSSO second sentence were noted as incoherent.

The GSSO:000485 definition was selected as the best available: Birgit Meldal proposed it; the group noted it as "the most comprehensive" and "the best option identified." Its key advantages: broad scope including all diseases, lesions, disorders, AND non-pathological conditions; examples provided (pregnancy, childbirth); suggests the norm of medical treatment. Acknowledged weaknesses: the second sentence ("Any kind of illness or circumstance which requires medical monitoring") is less coherent than the first sentence and was noted as potentially restrictive (colour blindness does not require monitoring). However, these were not blocking.

Alternative: schema.org MedicalCondition was supported by (Philippe Rocca-Serra) and partially by Ammar Ammar, but criticised for omitting non-pathological conditions and lacking the "medical" component.

**Outcome:** Consensus reached on GSSO:000485 as the preferred definition. Label `Medical_Condition` confirmed.
