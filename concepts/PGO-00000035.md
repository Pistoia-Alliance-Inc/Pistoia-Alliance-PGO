# Editorial notes

## Summary Expert Alignment *(dcterms:description)*

**Round 1** (2025-11-14)

After reviewing multiple definitions, the group reached clear consensus on a definition originating from the BioAssay Ontology (BAO). This definition was preferred because it describes the methodology or protocol used to perform an assay, rather than the assay execution or experimental outcome itself. While acknowledged as imperfect, it was considered the most complete and least ambiguous of the candidates.

Duplicate BAO entry (via BioPortal) flagged by Giovanni; the two rows referred to the same BAO_0003028 concept and one was hidden. Row 9 (NCIT "Measured Bioassay") rejected by Darko as too specific (relies on "featureExtraction event").

An expert expressed reservations: the BAO definition is limited to screening assays, and the purpose of the assay method (to measure presence, amount, or activity of an analyte) is missing. However, the group converged: Darko — "It covers roughly what assay method means... it's 80% there." Birgit — "The least of all the evils."

**Outcome:** Consensus reached on BAO BAO_0003028 as the recommended definition. Label `Assay_Method` confirmed.

---

**Round 2** (2026-05-07)

The group reviewed the Assay Method concept, which had been returned by the Steering Group after Round 1. The BAO definition was rejected by SG as circular (defining "assay method" using "assay") and as too narrow in scope (focused on bio-screening only). Multiple alternative definitions from OBI, CHEMINF, and a participant-proposed ICH-derived definition were considered, but no candidate satisfied all use cases.

The meeting ended without a recommended definition, with two foundational questions left unresolved: (1) whether Assay Method should be classified as an information content entity (a plan or document) or as a process class; and (2) whether a trusted, persistent source of assay method instances can be identified to anchor the concept.

Birgit Meldal's key insight: *"Method is how we do it, assay is what we do."* Philippe Rocca-Serra committed to sketching an OWL model to help resolve the ontological class question. All participants were asked to identify trusted sources and examples of instances offline.

**Recommendation from session:** Add `Experimental_Method` to the list of core concepts (potential interoperability bridge to CMC-P-O). NB: Assay could be a subclass of `Experimental_Method` at a higher level.

**Outcome:** No decision — further offline work required. Two blocking questions identified. Mini-model sketch session held separately (2026-05-06) with a smaller working group.

---

**Round 3 *(Mini-model working session)*** (2026-05-06)

Working group session focused on resolving the ontological modelling questions. The group sketched a mini-model placing Assay_Method in relation to adjacent concepts (see §6.4). The two blocking questions from Round 2 were addressed: (1) Assay_Method is the technique — not a process and not an information content entity per se, but the type of approach; (2) instances can be sourced from OBI and protocol repositories.

An amended BAO-derived definition was proposed by the group: *"The technique required to perform a part of an assay; includes assay design methods, assay supporting methods, laboratory methods."* This removes "screening" restriction and eliminates circularity.

**Outcome:** Revised definition proposed. Blocking questions resolved. Recommendation to proceed to SG Round 2 formulated at subsequent session (2026-06-04).

---

**Round 4 *(Definition resolved)*** (2026-06-04)

Final resolution session. The PGO-adapted BAO definition was confirmed as the recommended text. Both blocking questions from Round 2 resolved. ISSUE I-04 (trailing space in label) also resolved. Status updated from 7b to 6.

**Outcome:** Recommendation formulated for SG Round 2.
