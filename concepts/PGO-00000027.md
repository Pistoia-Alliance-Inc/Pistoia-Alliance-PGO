# Editorial notes

## Summary Expert Alignment *(dcterms:description)*

**Round 1** (2025-11-14)

The alignment sessions (2025-11-14 and 2025-11-26) explored the scope of Therapeutic/Medical Indication as a specialisation of Indication (PGO V1.0). The group noted that no single available definition was fully satisfactory: "There is no fully satisfactory candidate. A combination of the wording from e.g. https://schema.org/MedicalIndication and e.g. https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q496997 would be preferred."

The NCIT C41184 definition (already used for PGO V1.0 Indication) was excluded for re-use here. The EUPATI Toolbox definition was broadly rejected as drug-centric and lacking a persistent identifier. Wikipedia was qualitatively appreciated but rejected for instability.

Schema.org MedicalIndication emerged as the preferred primary source: Ammar Ammar proposed it; an expert noted its conciseness; Birgit Meldal endorsed it for including risk factors (relevant for vaccine indications); an expert found it "less verbose and more focused"; Philippe Rocca-Serra and Ammar Ammar gave explicit +1. Wikidata Q496997 was strongly endorsed as a complementary framing (Birgit Meldal, Philippe Rocca-Serra) but was not selected as primary given stability concerns.

A key label discussion emerged: the group strongly felt that "Therapeutic_Indication" is too narrow — it excludes diagnostic indications and prevention scenarios. The proposal to change the label to "Medical_Indication" was put forward during the 2025-11-26 session and confirmed by the SG at 2026-05-13 (ISSUE I-02). The meeting notes record: "Medical Indication is broader than Therapeutic Indication."

The relationship to PGO V1.0 Indication was discussed: Medical_Indication is likely a child entity of Indication, with a more specific therapy-oriented scope. This modelling point was not formally resolved at the expert level.

**Outcome:** Consensus reached on schema.org MedicalIndication as the preferred definition. Label changed to `Medical_Indication`. SG approval: 2026-05-13. Editorial note: definition may need future refinement incorporating Wikidata Q496997 wording.
