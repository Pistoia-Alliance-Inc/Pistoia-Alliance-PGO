# Editorial notes

## Summary Expert Alignment *(dcterms:description)*

**Round 1** (2024-11-21)

The session focused on selecting the most appropriate definition for the core concept "Cell" in PGO, with particular attention to scoping relative to related concepts.

Key discussion points:
- **Concept hierarchy**: "Cell" is the highest-level concept; "cell type" and "cell line" emerged as distinct concepts that should receive separate definitions in future PGO phases.
- **Multiple candidate definitions deemed acceptable**: All three candidates (NCIT_C12508, CL_0000000, EFO_0000324) were considered satisfactory for specific use cases.
- **Selection rationale**: NCIT_C12508 was preferred based on: (1) frequency of use in relevant biomedical applications; (2) its ability to anchor a generalisable, foundational biological concept; (3) ability to accommodate sub-concepts through PGO's planned LinkML framework.
- A mechanism for alternative definitions via LinkML was noted as a feature that would allow ontological flexibility and layered specificity.

**Outcome:** Consensus reached on NCIT_C12508. Formal recommendation submitted to the Steering Group.
