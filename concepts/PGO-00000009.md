# Definition *(skos:definition)*

A definite pathologic process with a characteristic set of signs and symptoms. It may affect the whole body or any of its parts, and its etiology, pathology, and prognosis may be known or unknown.

# Editorial notes

## Summary Expert Alignment *(dcterms:description)*

**Round 1** (2024-12-04)

The session evaluated three candidate definitions for "disease". The NCIT C2991 definition was initially recommended by multiple steering group members, but the expert group raised several concerns:
- Too generic — appears to encompass "condition" as well as "disease"
- The NCIT class hierarchy was considered better than other resources (a relevant advantage for integration)

The experts favoured the MeSH D004194 definition for its precision: *"A definite pathologic process with a characteristic set of signs and symptoms."*

Key recommendations:
1. Adopt MeSH D004194 as the definition of "disease".
2. Add a separate core concept "Condition" to PGO, with a distinct definition from "Disease".

Subsequent note (April 2025): "disease" and "condition" are not to be considered material entities ("things") or roles, but rather as "processes". This distinction may be relevant for future LinkML modelling.

**Outcome:** Consensus on MeSH D004194. Recommendation to add "Condition" as a Phase 2 concept. Submitted to Steering Group.
