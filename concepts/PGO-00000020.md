# Editorial notes

## Summary Expert Alignment *(dcterms:description)*

**Round 1** (2025-12-11)

The session (2025-12-11) covered Site and Study_Site (both concepts discussed together given their parent-child relationship — Site record: another concept).

For Study_Site, the group noted it adds necessary value as a non-clinical complement to Clinical_Study_Site (already in PGO V1.0). An expert confirmed relevance for preclinical settings. The key conceptual insight came from an expert and Christine: study sites must be **approved** — *"you have to go through a process to get approval for a place to actually be a study site."* This "approved" qualifier was seen as fundamental: it distinguishes Study_Site from any arbitrary research location, reflects regulatory reality, and is absent from the NCIT facility-framing and CDISC investigator-framing alternatives.

Three main NCIT-sourced definitions were reviewed:
1. NCIT C80403 primary formulation ("A facility in which study/protocol activities are conducted") — noted by Darko (from Phase 1 history) as too restrictive given "facility" scope.
2. CDISC formulation ("The location at which a study investigator conducts study activities") — rejected for requiring a named "study investigator" and being too study-agent-centric.
3. MRCT-Ctr formulation ("An approved location where research study activities take place") — preferred by an expert, an expert, and Christine for "approved" qualifier and broader "research study" (not "clinical study") scope. Giovanni: *"Study site. This goes to light."*

**Outcome:** Consensus reached on the NCIT C80403 / MRCT-Ctr formulation as the preferred definition. Label `Study_Site` confirmed.
