# Definition *(skos:definition)*

A laboratory test or analysis of the biological activity of a substance performed by studying its effect on an organism or in a test tube under controlled conditions.

# Editorial notes

## Summary Expert Alignment *(dcterms:description)*

**Round 1** (2025-11-14)

The session reviewed six candidate definitions for Bioassay. The discussion established that Bioassay should be understood as a narrower child of Assay (PGO-00000001), with the key distinguishing feature being the biological substrate — the assay measures biological activity on a living organism or in a controlled in vitro system.

The BAO and MMO definitions were challenged for reading as assay method definitions (describing how to run an assay) rather than definitions of what a bioassay is. The MIRO definition was criticized for limiting scope to organism effects only, excluding in vitro contexts. The MeSH definition suffered from the same method-framing problem.

The NCIT C16341 definition — proposed by — was favoured as the clearest available option: it covers both in vivo (organism) and in vitro (test tube) scope, and is concise without over-specifying the method. Acknowledged limitations: "test tube" is an informal proxy for in vitro; the scope of one vs. multiple substances in a single assay was flagged as ambiguous but not blocking. The group noted that "assay" is always artificial (not in situ with a patient). Consensus was reached on NCIT C16341.

**Outcome:** Consensus reached on NCIT C16341 as the preferred definition. Label `Bioassay` confirmed. Open discussion: "an expert's input is key" for any future refinement; precision on in vitro vs in vivo scope was flagged for potential improvement.
