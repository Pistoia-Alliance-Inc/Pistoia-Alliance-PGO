# Editorial notes

## Summary Expert Alignment *(dcterms:description)*

**Round 1** (2025-05-08)

The session aimed to reach consensus on the appropriate ontological definition for the concept "Assay" (or "Bioassay") within PGO, and to settle the preferred label.

A key conceptual discussion clarified the semantic boundaries between related terms:
- **Assay** — a planned process measuring a specific activity or property (biological, chemical, physicochemical, sequence-based), typically following a defined protocol.
- **Experiment** — a broader, less formalized concept that may include multiple assays.
- **Study** — may encompass multiple experiments or be used synonymously with experiment.
- **Run** — repeated executions of the same assay or experiment.
- **Bioassay** — a subset of assays involving biological materials, reagents, or measuring biological properties.
- **Test** — sometimes used interchangeably with assay in regulatory/pharmacopeial contexts.

Four candidate definitions were evaluated:
1. **OBI_0000070** ("A planned process with the objective to produce information about the material entity that is the evaluant, by physically examining it or its proxies.") — considered sufficiently flexible and formally grounded. Ultimately adopted.
2. **NCIT_C16341** (Bioassay) — positively received by multiple experts (Peter McQuilton, Birgit Meldal) for comprehensiveness but links to the PGO concept "Substance", creating potential circular dependency.
3. **BAO_15** — proposed by 2+ partners; criticized as overly complex and including SOP-level information.
4. **NCIT_C60819** — considered too narrow, limited to amount or drug-effect measurement.

The group also resolved the label question: **"Assay"** is preferred over "Bioassay" or "Assay/Bioassay" for clarity and generality. "Bioassay" may be considered as a separate concept in future PGO versions.

**Outcome:** Consensus reached on OBI_0000070 as the preferred definition with the label "Assay". Formal recommendation submitted to the Steering Group.
