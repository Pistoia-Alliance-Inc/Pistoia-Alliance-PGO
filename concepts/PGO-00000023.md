# Editorial notes

## Summary Expert Alignment *(dcterms:description)*

**Round 1** (2025-10-30)

The 2025-10-30 session (jointly covering Instrument and Production_Equipment) established the key organising principle for Instrument: instruments "create data." This became the central discriminator against the broader Device concept.

Multiple candidate definitions were reviewed. Definitions from EFO, Allotrope, OBI, JERM, and MODSCI were rejected for being either too broad (equating instrument with any device) or too domain-specific (limited to laboratory or mass spectrometry contexts). The SCO/VSTOI definition — "a device or mechanism that is used to acquire attribute values of entities of interest" — was noted as directionally correct by (Darko Hric) but not ultimately preferred.

The MIRAPIE definition — "Measuring tool that creates data points, e.g., thermometer and other sensors or wearable devices" — was selected as the closest match to the group's intended scope: it focuses on data creation without over-specifying the how, is general enough to cover lab instruments, sensors, wearables, and survey instruments, and provides concrete examples without being restrictive. The SG confirmed in their vote that the scope explicitly includes questionnaires and survey instruments, not only physical laboratory instruments.

**Outcome:** Consensus reached on the MIRAPIE definition. Label `Instrument` confirmed.
