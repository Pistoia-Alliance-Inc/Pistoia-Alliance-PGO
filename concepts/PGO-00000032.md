# Editorial notes

## Summary Expert Alignment *(dcterms:description)*

**Round 1** (2026-04-23)

The session compared two candidate definitions for Cell_Type, from the Cell Ontology (CL) and the Experimental Factor Ontology (EFO).

The experts rejected the CL definition by consensus as hard to understand and too arbitrary in how it groups cells. The discussion raised a modeling point: a cell type is a descriptor of a cell (a class), not a material entity, which matters for placement in ontologies aligned with Basic Formal Ontology (BFO).

All experts accepted the EFO definition, "a distinct morphological or functional form of cell" (examples: epithelial cell, glial cell). The group chose not to add detail, because a more specific wording could exclude valid instances. The question of when to name a concept with the suffix "type" (cell type, tissue type) was noted as an open modeling topic for later phases.

**Outcome:** Consensus reached on the EFO definition (EFO_0000324). Label `Cell_Type` confirmed.
