# Definition *(skos:definition)*

A site is a spatial region bounded (in part or in whole) by material entities and may be occupied by material entities.

# Editorial notes

## Summary Expert Alignment *(dcterms:description)*

**Round 1** (2025-12-11)

The 2025-12-11 session (jointly covering Site and Study_Site) reviewed five candidate definitions for Site. Birgit Meldal raised a conceptual question: is "site" as a parent concept useful as a metadata determinant, or would it be better to only define the specific subtypes (Study_Site, Clinical_Study_Site, etc.)? The group resolved that the parent concept adds value for hierarchy clarity and future modelling.

The BFO formal definition (BFO_0000029) was noted as a logical axiom rather than a prose definition, making it unsuitable as a standalone PGO definition. The GENEPIO/BFO-attributed definition was appreciated for explicitly covering both material and immaterial entities and including body-part examples, but Giovanni Nisato flagged provenance uncertainty (it appears to be an EFO-added definition adopted by GENEPIO, not the actual BFO definition). The OMG Commons definition was well received by the group but could not be formally cited as its URL was not yet resolvable at the time of the meeting (~6-8 weeks pending OMG publication).

The SIO definition (SIO_000019) emerged as the slight group preference: clear prose definition, traceable resolvable URI, and no conflicting definition history. Birgit Meldal endorsed it for breadth; Ammar Ammar noted it is "clearer and has a traceable URI." Darko Hric gave a positive assessment. The main concern raised was whether "bounded … by material entities" applies to all site types (e.g. a country's border might not be a material entity). This was noted but not blocking.

**Outcome:** Slight preference for SIO_000019 definition recorded. Label `Site` confirmed.
