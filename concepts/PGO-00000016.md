# Editorial notes

## Summary Expert Alignment *(dcterms:description)*

**Round 1** (2025-03-06)

The session jointly evaluated "Substance" and "Compound". Key findings:

- **IDMP-O Substance**: The definition appears to be an "information or specification entity" rather than a material entity — a concern flagged for potential IDMP-O engagement (question: what is the rationale for choosing a specification entity over a material entity?).
- "Substance" has a customary meaning as a functional, contextual term for relatively homogeneous materials (e.g., plastic, wood, tea).
- **NCIT_C45306** emerged as the consensus candidate: three partners indicated use; endorsed by CS, BM, PMQ.

Cross-concept evaluation:
- "Compound" has more precise chemical composition than "Substance"; "Chemical compound" may be a more accurate label for compound.
- Some "substances" may also be "compounds" or "drugs" — semantic overlap due to usage.
- Not all "substances" are "compounds" or "drugs" — there is merit in retaining "substance" as a distinct core concept.
- Expert group tasked with providing examples of substances that are not compounds or drugs.

**Outcome:** Consensus on NCIT_C45306. The group agreed to: (1) evaluate "substance" in conjunction with "compound" — done on 2025-03-06; (2) evaluate in conjunction with "product" — done on 2025-05-23.
