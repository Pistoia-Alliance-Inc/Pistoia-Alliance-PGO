# Definition *(skos:definition)*

A production equipment is a specialization of a saref:Device and s4bldg:PhysicalObject that can produce items in a manufacturing process. This class represents an individual production equipment device and includes their specification in terms of functions, states and services.

# Editorial notes

## Summary Expert Alignment *(dcterms:description)*

**Round 1** (2025-10-30)

The 2025-10-30 session (jointly covering Instrument and Production_Equipment) reviewed candidate definitions for manufacturing/production equipment. The core quality criterion established by the group: the definition should cover instruments or tools used in manufacturing processes without limiting scope to biomedical applications.

The ENVO definition was immediately rejected for a circular argument — calling equipment a "manufactured product." The CRISP biomedical equipment definition was rejected as too domain-narrow. The IOF "Piece of equipment" definition was considered positively by for semantic adaptability but rejected because it is not self-contained (requires "equipment role" defined separately).

The SAREF4INMA ProductionEquipment definition was preferred: Darko Hric and gave +1. Pros: domain-specific, aligned with IoT and manufacturing standards, provides a class model for subtypes. Cons: relies on SAREF context. The group noted a critical follow-up: CMC compatibility at Pistoia Alliance must be verified before formal adoption.

Post-session note (2026-05-28): the SAREF model reference in the definition (saref:Device) was flagged as non-freestanding; a definition cleanup was initiated to document the adoption as 'adapted from SAREF4INMA' and to remove or qualify the SAREF model reference. An open action notes that future revision may replace saref:Device references with PGO entities as the ontology matures.

**Outcome:** Consensus reached on SAREF4INMA ProductionEquipment definition. Label changed to `Production_Equipment` (from temp label `Manufacturing_Equipment`) at SG approval.
